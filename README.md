# CoGo – Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 6, 2026**

Also see: [Terms of Use](./TERMS.md)

## 1. Introduction

CoGo ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CoGo Android application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By creating an account or using the App you agree to this policy and our [Terms of Use](./TERMS.md).

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

- **Account data** — full name, email address, gender, and an optional profile photograph you provide at registration or later from the Profile screen.
- **Location data** — your approximate or precise location, used while you search for or publish a ride. We do not track location continuously in the background. When you use location features, we may store approximate coordinates on your account so we can notify you about nearby new rides.
- **Ride data** — pickup and drop-off locations, departure time, seat preferences, vehicle details you enter, and journey history.
- **Communications** — in-app chat messages and images exchanged with other CoGo users for a ride.
- **Reviews** — optional ratings and written reviews after a completed ride. These are publicly visible to other CoGo users.
- **Device data** — Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token used solely to deliver push notifications to your device.
- **Diagnostics** — crash logs and stability data via Firebase Crashlytics (associated with your account ID when signed in), and anonymised app interaction data via Firebase Analytics.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- Create and maintain your account.
- Match drivers with passengers on shared routes and enforce women-only ride eligibility.
- Display your name, rating, gender (where relevant), and profile photo to other users you interact with.
- Send push notifications for ride requests, booking confirmations, cancellations, departure-time changes, chat messages, rating reminders, and other in-app events.
- Calculate and display public driver/passenger ratings after completed rides.
- Diagnose crashes and improve the App through analytics and Crashlytics.

We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties.

## 4. Data Sharing

We share data only with:

- **Other CoGo users** — your name, profile photo, rating, public reviews, and ride-related details needed to book and travel together. Fellow passengers on an accepted ride can see who else is travelling.
- **Google / Firebase** — our backend infrastructure (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics). Governed by [Firebase's Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / OSRM** — place-search text and coordinates are sent to these geocoding and routing services. No account password is included.

## 5. Data Retention

- Your account and profile data are retained as long as your account is active.
- Ride records and reviews are retained to support ratings, history, and safety of the marketplace.
- Chat messages and chat images for a ride are deleted when that ride is marked completed or cancelled.
- Read in-app notifications older than 72 hours are automatically deleted. Optional rating prompts may remain until you act on them or they are cleaned up by our systems.
- Crashlytics and Analytics data are retained according to Google Firebase product policies.

## 6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

- Update your name and profile photo from the Profile screen in the App.
- Delete your account from the Profile screen (you will be asked to re-enter your password). This deletes your Firebase Auth account, profile document, and notification inbox. Some ride or review records that other users rely on may remain in residual form.
- Email us (see Section 9) if you need help with deletion; we aim to respond within 30 days.
- Revoke location or notification permission through your device's app settings. Ride search, publish, and nearby-ride alerts require location; push alerts require notification permission.

## 7. Children's Privacy

CoGo is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

## 8. Security

All data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure, which uses industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to Firestore and Storage is gated by Firebase Authentication — only authenticated users can read or write data under our security rules. Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
